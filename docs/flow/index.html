Bardic Flow

Add this page to your Home Screen

This page is the app. Add it to your Home Screen: it becomes the Bardic Flow icon, and you play from there.

First, open this page in Safari. The browser you are in cannot add apps to the Home Screen.

Tap its menu and choose Open in Safari (or Open in browser), or copy the link and paste it in Safari.

  1. arthurvangeersdaele.github.io
    Tap Share
  2. CopyAdd to Reading ListAdd to Home Screen
    Tap Add to Home ScreenScroll down the list if needed.
  3. CancelAdd Bardic Flow Open as Web App
    Tap AddIf you see Open as Web App, keep it on.
  4. Play from the Bardic Flow iconClose the browser, open Bardic Flow from your Home Screen and hold your phone upright.

Only once: iOS lets an app be added from a page through these steps only.
