Bardic Discovery edition

Choose a story

Four ready-made stories to run at your table. Opening a story loads every chapter, so each sound starts the moment you tap it.

This is a discovery edition. Try Bardic with four fixed stories, straight from your browser. It needs a connection, loads a whole story before playing, and stops if the page closes.

The Android app is made for real sessions: your own sounds and stories, a low-latency engine, playback with the screen off, and everything stays on your phone.

Music and sounds: Battle for Wesnoth, 0 A.D., Flare and Stendhal (credits).
