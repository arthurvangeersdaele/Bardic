Recueil

Tales left behind by the bards of the Compagnie. Open one, take it to your table.

    Add your tale

    1. Forge your story in the Story builder or in the Bardic app.
    2. Lay the .bardic file in the Recueil on GitHub. You only need a GitHub account: GitHub prepares the pull request for you.
    3. Once it is accepted, your tale appears here for every bard.
