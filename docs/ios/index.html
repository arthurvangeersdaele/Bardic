Made for iPhone and tablets

This edition of Bardic installs as an app on the Home Screen of an iPhone, an iPad or an Android tablet. Open this page on one of them to install it.

On an Android phone, the Bardic app is your table: download it from the Bardic page. On a computer, the web edition and the Compagnie des bardes are waiting for you.

Bardic for iPhone

Add this page to your Home Screen

This page is the app. Add it to your Home Screen: it becomes the Bardic icon, and you play from there.

First, open this page in Safari. The browser you are in cannot add apps to the Home Screen.

Tap its menu and choose Open in Safari (or Open in browser), or copy the link and paste it in Safari.

One tap, then open Bardic from your Home Screen. Or follow the steps below.
  1. arthurvangeersdaele.github.io
    Tap Share
  2. CopyAdd to Reading ListAdd to Home Screen
    Tap Add to Home ScreenScroll down the list if needed.
  3. CancelAdd Bardic Open as Web App
    Tap AddIf you see Open as Web App, keep it on.
  4. Play from the Bardic iconClose the browser, open Bardic from your Home Screen and turn it sideways.

Only once: iOS lets an app be added from a page through these steps only.
