Android app in development

Your table deserves a soundtrack.

Bardic turns your phone into a sound desk for tabletop RPGs. Prepare music, ambience and events for each chapter of your story, then play them live with one hand while you run the game.

Gain Bardic Inspiration
On a computer, join the Compagnie des bardes and play online: tales from the Recueil, your own .bardic files, and a builder to forge new ones.

The web version is a discovery edition: four ready-made stories, nothing to install. The Android app, in development, is the one built for real sessions, with your own sounds and stories, a low-latency engine and playback with the screen off. Engine test build (APK)

Three zones

Everything a scene needs, stacked.

Each chapter opens on a landscape mixing table split into three rows. Scroll each row sideways to reach its sound cards.

Music

One track at a time. Starting a new one crossfades from the current track automatically.

Ambience

Rain, fire, wind, crowds. Layer as many as you like, usually on infinite loop.

Events

Church bells, a full battle, a volcano erupting. Short or long, triggered when the story calls for it.

Features

Built for the heat of the game.

Sound cards

Play, pause, fade-out end, volume, and a loop mode per card: once, twice or forever.

See the end coming

Progress bars shift from green to red, with a countdown in the last stretch, so a sound never cuts out by surprise.

Up to 20 sounds at once

A low-latency native audio engine (Oboe) mixes everything into one stream, without lag.

Soundbox

Level-ups, explosions, critical hits: a shared pad of quick effects, reachable from every table. Pin your favorites.

Master controls

One volume, one play/pause and one stop for the whole table. Keeps playing with the screen off.

Stories & chapters

Prepare each session ahead of time. Chapters are locked during play, so nothing moves by accident.

Share your campaigns

Export a story as a single .bardic file, sounds included, and import it on another phone.

Four themes

Parchment, Obsidian, Blood and Divination, each in light and dark.

Your sounds

Bring your own library.

Bardic plays your own MP3 files. Import them from the app, or drop them from a computer into a folder on your phone.
