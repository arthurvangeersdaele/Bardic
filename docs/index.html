Your table deserves a soundtrack.

Bardic turns your phone into a sound desk for tabletop RPGs. Prepare music, ambiances and events for each chapter of your story, then play them live with one hand while you run the game.

Bardic Key Principles

The Barde tells a Story.

A Story has its Chapters.

Chapters have theirs own Sounds.

Prepare your story before your session. At the table, each chapter opens its own mixing table, and every sound waits on a card, ready to fire under your hand.

3 sort of Sounds

Music: one track at a time, crossfading. Ambiances: rain, fire, crowds, layered on loop. Events: bells, roars, explosions, on cue or at random.

Community

The Compagnie des bardes.

Every table has its tales, and some of them deserve to travel. Forge your story into a .bardic file, with its music, its ambiences and its thunderclaps, and lay it in the Recueil for every game master to find. Then bring one of theirs home to your table tonight.

RecueilThe book of tales shared by the bards. Download one, or play it right away. Play a taleOpen a .bardic file from your computer on the mixing table. Web story builderCreate your story from MP3s or a YouTube clip, and forge your .bardic file.

On your phone, the Bardic app is your forge and your table. The builder and Play a tale wait for you on a computer.

Under the hood

Open source, all the way down.

Every line of Bardic lives on GitHub: the Android app, the iPhone web app, this site, the file format and the build pipeline. Read it, fork it, open an issue, or send a tale to the Recueil.

arthurvangeersdaele/BardicSource, issues and releases 0Android APK downloads

Android Kotlin, C++

  • Jetpack Compose UI, landscape only.
  • C++ mixer on Oboe (AAudio, OpenSL ES): one low-latency stream, up to 20 voices, no locks or allocation on the audio thread.
  • NDK AMediaCodec decoding into lock-free ring buffers: every loaded sound starts instantly, loops are gapless.
  • Soundbox effects decoded in memory, up to 24 one-shots on top.

iPhone and web JavaScript

  • iPhone: a Home Screen web app, stories in IndexedDB, offline thanks to a service worker.
  • Web player on the Web Audio API, sounds preloaded as blobs.
  • Story builder: everything in the browser, MP3 encoding with LAME, a YouTube clip recorded from the tab's own sound.

.bardic format 2

  • A stored ZIP: story.json and the audio files.
  • Signed with a bard name, versioned by a hidden revision counter.
{ "format": "bardic", "version": 2, "bard": "Chiffon", "revision": 3, "chapters": [ { "title": "The flooded nave", "music": [...], "ambience": [...], "events": [...] } ], "soundbox": [...] }

Pipeline GitHub Actions

  • Every push to main builds the APK and publishes it as the latest beta; the app offers the update itself.
  • Tales sent to the Recueil are checked on their pull request, then indexed automatically.
  • Sounds of the web stories come from open source games (Battle for Wesnoth, 0 A.D., Flare, Stendhal), credited file by file.
