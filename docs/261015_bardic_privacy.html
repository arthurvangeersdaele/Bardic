Privacy policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

In short: Bardic has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. You don't need an account. Your stories and sounds stay on your phone. The only personal data Bardic ever handles is your public GitHub username, and only if you choose to connect GitHub to register a bard name.

This policy covers the Bardic app for Android, the Bardic web app for iPhone and the Bardic website (web player, Story builder and Recueil). Bardic is an open source project made by Arthur Vangeersdaele. Its whole source code is public on GitHub, so anything written here can be checked line by line.

What stays on your device

The stories, chapters and sounds you create or import are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage (or in your browser's storage for the iPhone web app). They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

When you export a story as a .bardic file, the file is created on your device and goes wherever you decide to send it. Bardic does not keep a copy elsewhere.

What Bardic downloads

Bardic connects to the internet only to download things you ask for:

  • Discovery stories and the Recueil (the library of shared tales) are downloaded from the Bardic website, hosted on GitHub Pages.
  • Sounds from a link you paste yourself are fetched from that link.

Like any website, the servers that answer these requests (GitHub, or the site behind a link you paste) may record technical information such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. Bardic itself does not collect or store this information. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Connecting with GitHub (optional)

In the settings, you can connect a GitHub account to register a bard name that signs your stories. This is never required: without it, your stories are simply signed "Forgotten Barde".

If you connect:

  • You sign in on github.com with a short code. Bardic asks for no GitHub permission and only learns your public GitHub username and account id.
  • A sign-in token is kept on your device so you stay connected. You can disconnect at any time in the settings.
  • The bard name you register is linked to your GitHub username in a public registry file in the Bardic GitHub repository, so that nobody else can sign stories with your name.
  • The sign-in goes through a small Bardic helper hosted on Cloudflare Workers, which relays the request to GitHub and updates the registry. It does not keep any other record.

Sharing a tale in the Recueil

Sending a tale to the Recueil is done by you, through a pull request on GitHub. Everything you send there (the .bardic file, its sounds and your bard name) becomes public, under GitHub's terms. Only send sounds you have the right to share.

What Bardic does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting service, no tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone.
  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties.

Bardic keeps playing with the screen off by running a media playback service. This only plays your sounds and sends nothing anywhere.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Bardic, or deleting a story in the app, removes that data from your device. To remove your bard name from the public registry, disconnect GitHub in the settings and open an issue asking for its removal.

Children

Bardic is a tool for game masters and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone who does not choose to connect a GitHub account.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. For any question about your privacy, open an issue on GitHub.
