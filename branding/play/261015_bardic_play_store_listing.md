## Full description (max 4000)

```
Your table deserves a soundtrack.

Bardic turns your phone into a sound desk for tabletop RPGs. Prepare music, ambiances and events for each chapter of your story, then play them live with one hand while you run the game.

THE BARDE TELLS A STORY
A Story has its Chapters. Chapters have their own Sounds.
Prepare your story before your session. At the table, each chapter opens its own mixing table, and every sound waits on a card, ready to fire under your hand.

THREE KINDS OF SOUNDS
Music: one track at a time, crossfading.
Ambiances: rain, fire, crowds, layered on loop.
Events: bells, roars, explosions, on cue or at random.
And a Soundbox for each story, with quick effects always within reach.

YOU'RE IN CONTROL
Every sound card has everything it needs during the game, within reach of a thumb:
Play and pause at once, without a click.
Fade out softly instead of cutting.
Loop once, twice or forever. Events and ambiances can also come back at random.
Its own volume, under the master volume.
Time left that turns red in the last ten seconds.
A glow in its row's colour while it plays.
Preset everything before your session, and keep your eyes on your players.

BUILT FOR REAL SESSIONS
Your own stories and sounds, kept on your phone.
Playback keeps going with the screen off.
Up to 20 sounds loaded per chapter, gapless loops, instant start, on a low-latency audio engine.
Landscape layout, made for a phone lying next to your notes.

THE COMPAGNIE DES BARDES
Every table has its tales, and some of them deserve to travel. Forge your story into a .bardic file, with its music, its ambiances and its thunderclaps, and share it in the Recueil for every game master to find. Then bring one of theirs home to your table tonight.

OPEN SOURCE, ALL THE WAY DOWN
Every line of Bardic lives on GitHub. No ads, no tracking, no account needed.
Read it, fork it, open an issue, or send a tale to the Recueil:
https://github.com/arthurvangeersdaele/Bardic
```
